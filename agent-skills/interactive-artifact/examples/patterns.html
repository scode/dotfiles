1. TL;DR summary

Lead a page with the conclusion when the reader may stop after one paragraph. One per page, at the top.

Switching the queue to SQLite removes the Redis dependency and keeps p99 latency under 5 ms at our current load. It does not scale past one writer, which we are nowhere near.

3. Option cards

Lay out two to four alternatives side by side when you want the reader to pick one. Mark your recommendation, keep each card's pros and cons to the few that actually decide it. Add data-pick to the grid and data-value to each card to let the user pick one (see interactive.html).

Recommended

SQLite queue

One file next to the service, WAL mode, a single writer.

  • No new infrastructure
  • Transactional with the rest of our state
  • One writer process only

Redis streams

What we run today, via the shared cluster.

  • Already works
  • Scales to many writers
  • Cluster outages take us down
  • No transactions with our database

Postgres SKIP LOCKED

A jobs table polled with row locks.

  • Transactional
  • Adds load to the primary
  • Polling latency
